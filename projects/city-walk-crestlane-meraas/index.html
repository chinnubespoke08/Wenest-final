Overview

City Walk Crestlane by Meraas is a flagship urban waterfront residential destination in the heart of Dubai's iconic City Walk district. Designed around the calming theme of "water as the essence", Crestlane introduces mid-rise U-shaped architectural towers wrapped around serene central lagoons, floating amenities, and posh elevated parklands. Comprising a refined collection of 1 to 4-bedroom apartments, executive duplexes, and ultra-luxury penthouses, Crestlane blends cosmopolitan city energy with tranquil resort living.

The architectural approach balances sleek contemporary geometry with organic waterfront elements. Layered terraces, vertical shading fins, and floor-to-ceiling panoramic glass facades maximize natural light while framing direct views of the central water feature and the Downtown Dubai skyline. Interior spaces showcase 3.1-metre high ceilings, open-plan spatial flow, and premium natural materials including marble, timber parquet, and brushed metallic accents.

About the Property

City Walk Crestlane by Meraas offers a sophisticated urban living experience tailored to buyers seeking central Dubai location and architectural distinction. Each residence is delivered with fitted designer kitchens, integrated European appliances, stone countertops, custom cabinetry, and floor-to-ceiling sliding glass doors opening onto expansive private water-facing balconies. Duplex residences feature private elevator access and direct park entry, while penthouses provide double-height living areas and private rooftop plunge pools. Furthermore, all 1-bedroom and larger units qualify buyers for the 10-Year UAE Golden Visa, providing long-term residency and financial peace of mind.

Location

About the Location

City Walk is one of Dubai's most prestigious, pedestrian-friendly lifestyle districts — a vibrant urban enclave developed by Meraas that combines open-air shopping boulevards, gourmet restaurants, street art, and world-class entertainment venues such as the Coca-Cola Arena and Roxy Cinemas. Positioned just 3 minutes from Sheikh Zayed Road (E11), 7 minutes from Jumeirah Beach and The Dubai Mall, and 11 minutes from DIFC, Crestlane provides unmatched central accessibility for professionals, families, and high-net-worth investors. The district consistently records high occupancy rates and premium rental yields driven by executive tenant demand.
